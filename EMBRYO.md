# Embryo MicroPython

Embryo MicroPython is Embryo-Zero's fork of [micropython](https://github.com/micropython/micropython) (350908a, commit 350908a). It does Python on small chips.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
